Privacy Policy

DOITManager: Expense Tracker · Effective 20 October 2026

DOITManager is a personal expense tracker that works entirely on your device. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we do not collect any of your data.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server run by us or anyone else. We never see your transactions, balances, receipts or settings.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter is stored only on your device, inside the app: your transactions, accounts, categories, budgets, tags, projects, recurring entries and the receipt photos you attach. Your settings are stored on the device too, with the app lock kept in the device's secure storage. Deleting the app deletes the app's data from the device.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to send it, and only to the place you choose:

  • Backups. A backup is a file you create and then save or share yourself, for example to Files, iCloud Drive or email. Backups are encrypted by default with a recovery key or a passphrase you choose. You can turn encryption off yourself.
  • CSV export. A spreadsheet of your transactions, which you save or share yourself. It is not encrypted.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback, the app opens your own email or share sheet with your message and, if you include one, a screenshot. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Once you share a file, what happens to it is governed by the app or service you shared it with.

Device permissions

  • Camera: to photograph or scan a receipt for a transaction.
  • Photos: to pick a receipt image from your library, or to save a feedback screenshot when you ask.
  • Face ID / Touch ID: to unlock the app, if you turn the app lock on. The app never receives your biometric data; your device only tells it whether the unlock succeeded.
  • Notifications: local reminders on your device for recurring bills and amounts to confirm. They are scheduled by the app on the device; no notification server is involved.

Receipt text is read on the device, using the device's own text recognition. Images are not uploaded anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever collects data, this page and the app's App Store privacy details will say so before that version is released.
