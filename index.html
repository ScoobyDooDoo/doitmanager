DOITManager

Expense Tracker for iPhone and iPad

DOITManager keeps track of your spending, income, accounts, budgets and receipts, all on your own device.

For help, bug reports or suggestions, email wheremagichappen99@gmail.com. You can also send feedback from inside the app, with a screenshot attached.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your device. The app has no accounts and no servers, so we can't see or recover your data. Make backups from More → Backup & Restore.

How do I move to a new phone? Make a backup in the app, save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then use Restore from Backup in the app on the new phone. You need the recovery key or passphrase you chose for the backup.

How do I add the Quick-Add widget? Touch and hold the home screen, tap +, then choose DOITManager.
